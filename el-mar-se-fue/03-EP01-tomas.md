# EP 1 · Lista de tomas con prompts (3D)

**Duración total:** ~56 s · **9 tomas** · Formato 9:16
Cada toma: 1) crea la imagen con referencias → 2) anímala con el prompt de video.
`[ESTILO]` = bloque de estilo de `01-guia-estilo.md`.

---

### TOMA 1 · 0–3 s · El mar se va (GANCHO)
**Imagen:**
```
First-person POV looking down at own bare feet in shallow turquoise water on golden sand,
red string bracelet visible on left wrist at frame edge, the water is rapidly pulling away
from the feet leaving wet sand and small fish flopping, [ESTILO]
```
**Video:** `The water rapidly drains away from the feet toward the sea, sand ripples exposed, fish flopping, slight handheld camera shake, 3 seconds`
**Texto:** FICCIÓN · **Audio:** succión de agua, burbujas.

### TOMA 2 · 3–9 s · Nadie corre
**Imagen:**
```
First-person POV standing on a beach looking out at a huge exposed seabed where the ocean
has retreated hundreds of meters, stranded boats tilted on the sand, tourists laughing and
taking photos with their phones, kids running onto the wet seabed, golden hour, [ESTILO]
```
**Video:** `Slow pan across the empty seabed, tourists laughing and taking photos, kids running toward the exposed seafloor, 6 seconds`
**Texto:** "Nadie corre." · **Audio:** risas, fotos de celular.

### TOMA 3 · 9–13 s · Don Chuy avisa
**Imagen (ref: Don Chuy):**
```
Old fisherman with torn straw hat and white mustache running up the beach toward camera,
one arm raised, shouting in panic, tourists behind him confused, [ESTILO]
```
**Video:** `The old man runs toward the camera shouting with his arm raised, people turn to look at him, 4 seconds`
**Voz:** "¡TSUNAMI! ¡CORRAN A LA CAPILLA!"

### TOMA 4 · 13–19 s · ¿Dónde está Leo?
**Imagen (ref: Leo):**
```
First-person POV, wide view of the exposed seabed, far in the distance a small girl in a
yellow t-shirt with a star crouching and collecting seashells into a blue bucket, alone,
unaware, the sky starting to turn grey-green, [ESTILO]
```
**Video:** `Slow push-in toward the small girl far away on the empty seabed, she keeps picking up shells happily, clouds darken, 6 seconds`
**Texto:** "Ella no escuchó." · **Audio:** entra latido.

### TOMA 5 · 19–25 s · La línea blanca
**Imagen:**
```
First-person POV looking at the far horizon over the exposed seabed, a thin white line of
foam stretching across the entire horizon, growing taller, sky darkening, birds flying away
toward land, [ESTILO]
```
**Video:** `The white line on the horizon rises and grows, birds flee toward the camera, light dims, 6 seconds`
**Texto:** ⏱️ 4:00 · **Audio:** rugido grave lejano.

### TOMA 6 · 25–31 s · Dani te jala
**Imagen (ref: Dani + manos):**
```
First-person POV, Dani with short black hair and white linen shirt grabbing the viewer's
left arm with the red string bracelet, pulling toward a green hill with a white chapel,
terrified face, people running behind, [ESTILO]
```
**Video:** `Dani pulls the viewer's arm hard and screams, camera jerks toward the hill, crowd running, 6 seconds`
**Voz Dani:** "¡No llegas! ¡Vámonos, por favor!"

### TOMA 7 · 31–37 s · Leo te saluda (SILENCIO)
**Imagen (ref: Leo):**
```
First-person POV, medium shot of little girl Leo on the exposed seabed, turning toward the
camera, smiling innocently and waving with one hand, holding his blue bucket, completely
unaware, behind him on the horizon a massive dark wall of water, [ESTILO]
```
**Video:** `Leo turns, smiles and waves happily at the camera, the giant wave grows slowly behind him, 6 seconds`
**Audio:** **SILENCIO TOTAL 2 s**, luego solo latido.

### TOMA 8 · 37–43 s · La ola
**Imagen:**
```
Low angle shot of a gigantic tsunami wave taller than the beach hotels, dark blue water
with white foam at the crest, casting a huge shadow over the beach, tiny people running,
[ESTILO]
```
**Video:** `The massive wave rises and advances, its shadow covers the beach, foam curls at the top, 6 seconds`
**Texto:** ⏱️ 2:30 · **Audio:** rugido muy fuerte.

### TOMA 9 · 43–56 s · DECISIÓN (pantalla partida)
**Imagen izquierda (ref: Leo):** Leo pequeño en el fondo marino, la ola detrás.
**Imagen derecha (ref: Dani):** Dani en la colina, extendiendo la mano hacia ti.
**Edición:** congela ambas, desatura todo menos Leo (amarillo) y la mano de Dani.
**Texto:**
- **A 🏃 Bajas por Leo**
- **B ⛰️ Subes con Dani**
- Final (negro): "Comenta A o B. Lo que gane, pasa."

**Audio:** tic tac + latido → corte seco.

## Orden de trabajo
1. Hojas de personaje (`02-personajes.md`).
2. Las 9 imágenes, revisando que Leo, Dani y la pulsera sean iguales.
3. Animar en Kling/Veo (5–6 s c/u, genera 2–3 versiones y elige).
4. Voces en ElevenLabs (español, emoción alta).
5. Edición en CapCut: texto, FICCIÓN, subtítulos, sonido.
